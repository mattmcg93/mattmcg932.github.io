Doughrock: AI Wildlife Trap Network

A network of Raspberry Pi–based computer-vision wildlife traps with real-time object detection, environmental sensing, and automated climate control. The Raspberry Pis use temperature and humidity sensors to control a separate heater and dehumidifier, with conditions shown on the Climate Dashboard. A solar-powered outdoor camera also provides live views of the house, with its battery level tracked alongside the climate data.
