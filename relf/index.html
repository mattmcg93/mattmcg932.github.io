What RELF does

RELF uses controlled multi-light imaging, computer vision and machine learning to recover latent fingerprint detail without contaminating the evidence. The system can estimate surface curvature remotely and correct geometric distortion before the recovered print is passed to conventional fingerprint matching workflows.

Contactless extractionPreserves the fingerprint for downstream forensic work, including potential DNA recovery.
Automatic curvature correctionCorrects distortion caused by cylindrical and spherical surfaces without physically measuring them.
Hard surfaces, not easy demosDemonstrated on curved, specular and transparent objects such as glass lightbulbs, jars and chrome taps.
